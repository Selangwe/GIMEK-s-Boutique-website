The Boutique

Where Elegance Meets Comfort

GIMEK’s Boutique is a fashion destination in Yaoundé, built on a simple belief: you should never have to choose between looking beautiful and feeling good.

Every piece on our racks is chosen by hand, for the way it falls, the way it wears through a long Yaoundé afternoon, and the way it makes the person wearing it stand a little taller. Fashion, bags, shoes, accessories and beauty, gathered in one warm room on Rue de Kondengui.

Come in, take your time, try things on. We’re here to help you find the version of your style that feels most like you.
